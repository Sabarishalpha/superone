Our Process

Quality Assurance Process

Every component goes through a rigorous multi-stage quality process before delivery.

1

Drawing Review

Detailed review of customer drawings and specifications before production start

2

First Article Inspection

100% dimensional inspection of first article before bulk production approval

3

In-Process Checks

Regular in-process dimensional and visual inspection at critical stages

4

Final Inspection

100% final inspection with CMM and precision instruments before dispatch

5

Documentation

Inspection report and certificate of conformance provided with every delivery

Inspection

Quality Testing Capabilities

CMM Inspection

ZEISS Contura CMM with measuring volume 700×1000×600mm for precise 3D dimensional verification of complex components.

Surface Roughness Testing

Mitutoyo SJ-210 surface roughness tester for Ra/Rz measurements ensuring specified surface finish requirements.

Dimensional Gauging

Comprehensive set of micrometers, vernier calipers, bore gauges, and plug gauges covering all standard measurement ranges.

Visual Inspection

Systematic visual inspection with illuminated magnifiers and inspection tables for surface defect detection and finish verification.

Statistical Process Control

SPC implementation for critical dimensions using control charts and process capability studies (Cp, Cpk analysis).

Traceability & Documentation

Complete inspection records, material certificates, and Certificates of Conformance provided with every delivery batch.
