Our Facility

Purpose-Built for Precision

CNC Machining Area

Primary production floor housing 5 advanced CNC machining centers operating across 3 shifts. Equipped with auto-tool changers and high-speed spindles for maximum productivity.

Quality Inspection Bay

Dedicated quality lab with ZEISS CMM (Coordinate Measuring Machine), surface roughness testers, micrometers, bore gauges, and precision calibrated instruments.

Tool & Fixture Storage

Organized tool crib with climate-controlled storage for precision tooling, fixtures, gauges, and measuring instruments ensuring optimal tool life and performance.

Raw Material Store

Systematic raw material storage with bar stocks, billets, forgings, and castings organized by material grade. FIFO inventory management ensures material traceability.

Dispatch & Packaging

Dedicated dispatch area with proper packaging facilities ensuring components are protected and delivered safely. Includes rust prevention and VCI packaging for metal parts.

Engineering Office

Modern engineering office with CAD/CAM workstations for programming, design review, and process planning. Connected to shop floor for real-time production monitoring.
