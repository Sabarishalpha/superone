Our Engineering Process

A systematic, customer-focused approach that ensures every project is delivered on time and to exact specification.

Our Process Method

Manufacturing Workflow from Enquiry to Production

At Aarman Automation, every component moves through a disciplined, customer-first process designed to deliver reliable production results, exact specifications and fast sample approval.

1

Enquiry

Customer sends drawings, RFQ and technical requirements so our team can begin the project intake process immediately.

Fast response
2

Feasibility Assessment

Engineering evaluates drawings, tolerances, material selection and production feasibility for a robust manufacturing plan.

Technical review
3

Quotation

We provide a detailed quote with cost, lead time and process clarifications, ensuring transparency before work begins.

Clear pricing
4

Receipt of Sample Order

Once the sample order is confirmed, we start order registration and prepare the production schedule.

Order tracking
5

Process Planning

Our team defines machining strategy, cutting sequence, fixtures and tooling to deliver the ideal manufacturing path.

Production roadmap
6

Tooling, Fixtures & Gauges Preparation

Precision tooling, bespoke fixtures and calibration gauges are readied to maintain consistent quality during sample runs.

Quality setup
7

Sample Production

The first sample is manufactured under controlled conditions to validate the process and establish part quality.

Controlled trial
8

Sample Inspection

Every sample undergoes dimensional inspection, surface finish checks and quality review before submission.

Inspection ready
9

Sample Submission

Approved samples are shared with the customer along with inspection reports and manufacturing recommendations.

Report delivery
10

Sample Approval

Once customer approval is received, we finalize process specifications and confirm volume production readiness.

Approval gateway
11

Production Started

Full-scale production begins with repeatable CNC machining, quality monitoring and on-time delivery planning.

Begin production
