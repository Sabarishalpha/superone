Precision Manufacturing Across Industries

Our machining capabilities and quality standards make us the preferred partner for demanding applications across multiple sectors.

Automotive

Precision components for automotive OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers including engine, transmission, and chassis components.

  • Engine blocks and cylinder heads
  • Transmission housings
  • Differential components
  • Suspension and steering parts
  • Brake system components

Pumps & Valves

High-accuracy pump and valve components for industrial, chemical, and water treatment applications requiring tight sealing surfaces.

  • Pump casings and impellers
  • Valve bodies and seats
  • Stuffing box components
  • Shaft sleeves and wear rings
  • End plates and flanges

General Engineering

Custom machined components for general engineering applications including machine building, tooling, and industrial equipment manufacturing.

  • Machine tool components
  • Fixture and jig parts
  • Spindle and shaft components
  • Gear blanks and splines
  • Precision bushings

Defense & Heavy Engineering

High-reliability components for defense, heavy engineering, and critical infrastructure applications with strict documentation and traceability.

  • Structural components
  • Precision housings
  • Hydraulic components
  • Armament components
  • High-strength steel parts

Pharmaceutical & Food

Hygienic-grade SS304/316 components for pharmaceutical, food processing, and beverage equipment requiring polished surfaces and crevice-free design.

  • SS304 / SS316 components
  • Hygienic fittings
  • Tank accessories
  • Blender and mixer parts
  • Conveyor components

Packaging & Textile

Precision components for packaging machinery and textile equipment requiring consistent performance in high-cycle, continuous-duty applications.

  • Cam and cam followers
  • Knives and blades
  • Rollers and guides
  • Mounting flanges
  • Gear boxes and drives
